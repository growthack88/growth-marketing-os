# Problem interviews

## 1 · Recruiting

- Screen for the target customer, not for friendliness: one or two screener questions about recent behaviour ("In the last 3 months, did you [situation]?").
- Sources: niche Facebook, WhatsApp and Telegram groups, LinkedIn search, communities around adjacent tools, a small screener ad, people who complain in competitors' reviews.
- Offer something small and honest for their time (a gift card, early access). Never promise the product.
- Talk to one segment at a time. Mixed segments blur patterns.
- Check who pays and who uses it. If they're different people (employer vs. employee, parent vs. child), interview the payer too. Their pain decides the purchase.
- Don't name your solution or its angle (e.g. "healthy") in the questions. If they bring it up unprompted, write it down. That's signal.

## 2 · Script — English (20–30 min)

```
Intro (1 min)
"I'm researching how people handle [problem area]. I'm not selling anything today —
there are no right answers, and the most useful thing you can do is tell me what
actually happened."

Context (3 min)
- Tell me about your work / your week around [area].

The last time (10 min) — the core
- When was the last time [problem] happened? Walk me through it.
- What did you do next? Who else was involved?
- What did it cost you — time, money, stress, a client?
- How often does this happen?

Workarounds (7 min)
- What have you tried to solve it? (tools, people, spreadsheets, paying someone)
- What do you pay for that today, if anything?
- What did you stop using, and why?

Wrap-up (3 min)
- If you could wave a wand, what would be different?   (listen; don't pitch)
- Who else should I talk to?
- Can I follow up when there's something to try?
```

Never ask "Would you use / buy this?" or "Do you like this idea?" The answers are polite and predict nothing.

## 3 · Script — Egyptian Arabic (عامية)

```
مقدمة (دقيقة)
"أنا بعمل بحث عن إزاي الناس بتتعامل مع [المشكلة]. مش ببيع حاجة النهارده،
ومفيش إجابة صح أو غلط. أكتر حاجة هتفيدني إنك تحكيلي اللي حصل فعلًا."

السياق (3 دقايق)
- احكيلي عن شغلك أو يومك في الحتة دي.

آخر مرة (10 دقايق) — دي أهم حتة
- آخر مرة [المشكلة] حصلت كانت إمتى؟ احكيلي اللي حصل خطوة خطوة.
- عملت إيه بعدها؟ مين كان معاك في الموضوع؟
- كلفك إيه؟ وقت؟ فلوس؟ عميل؟
- بتحصل كل قد إيه؟

الحلول اللي جربتها (7 دقايق)
- جربت إيه عشان تحلها؟ (أدوات، حد بيساعدك، شيت، بتدفع لحد)
- بتدفع كام على ده دلوقتي؟
- إيه اللي بطّلت تستخدمه، وليه؟

الختام (3 دقايق)
- لو تقدر تغيّر حاجة واحدة، هتبقى إيه؟   (اسمع بس، متبيعش)
- تعرف حد تاني أكلمه؟
- ينفع أرجعلك لما يبقى فيه حاجة تجربها؟
```

For Gulf audiences, adapt the wording (e.g. «وش سويت بعدها؟», «كم كلفك؟») or use clear MSA. Ask once which the user prefers.

## 4 · Synthesis grid (`synthesize`)

Fill one row per interview, then read across the columns:

| # | Segment | Recent incident? (unprompted / prompted / none) | Frequency | Cost (time / money) | Workaround today | Paid for a workaround? | Exact phrases (verbatim) | Surprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then report:
1. **Against the rule:** how many described an unprompted recent incident AND a workaround → pass / fail / in between.
2. **Top 3 pains**, ranked by frequency × cost, each with verbatim quotes.
3. **Language bank:** the customer's own words for the problem. These become headlines and ad hooks in the smoke test.
4. **Segments that didn't care.** Exclude them from the smoke-test audience.
5. **Open questions** for the next round.

Never turn a handful of interviews into percentages for a market claim. Report counts ("7 of 10").
