# Test assets: what to write for each step

Write every asset out in full, in the user's language and dialect. Use the customer's own phrases from the interviews. **Never** add fake reviews, invented user counts, fake scarcity or claims the product can't back yet.

## 1 · Smoke-test landing page

```
HEADLINE        The outcome, in the customer's words from interviews (not the feature).
SUBHEAD         Who it's for + what changes for them. One sentence.
3 BULLETS       What they'll get — concrete, no superlatives.
HONEST STATUS   "We're launching soon. Join the waitlist to get early access [and the founding price]."
FORM            Email (plus WhatsApp number only if you'll actually use it). One button.
BUTTON          Action + benefit: "Get early access", not "Submit".
THANK-YOU PAGE  Confirms the sign-up, and asks ONE question: "What made you sign up?" (free text)
                or a single-choice question about the riskiest assumption.
```

Checklist before spending: message match with the ad, loads fast on mobile, sign-up event tracked once, a privacy note on the form, and no claims you can't keep.

Arabic example of the structure (Egyptian):

```
العنوان: العميل بيطنّش؟ [المنتج] يفكّره بالفلوس بدالك.
الوصف: للفريلانسرز اللي زهقوا من مطاردة العملاء على واتساب.
• فاتورة واضحة في دقيقة
• تذكير تلقائي في المعاد اللي تحدده
• تعرف مين دفع ومين لسه
الحالة: بنطلق قريب. سجّل وخد أولوية الدخول.
الزرار: عايز أولوية الدخول
```

## 2 · Smoke-test ads

- Write 3–5 variants. Each one tests a **different promise or angle** from the interview language bank, not small wording tweaks.
- Each ad must promise exactly what the page delivers (message match).
- Targeting: the segment that passed Step 1. Exclude existing followers and customers so the test measures strangers.
- Budget per variant: enough to reach the sample in [calculations](calculations.md). Don't split the budget so thin that no variant reaches it.

## 3 · Fake door

```
PLACEMENT   Where the real choice happens: a new plan on the pricing page, a button in the
            product, a menu item. Same visual weight as the options next to it.
ON CLICK    "[Feature] isn't live yet. We're checking whether to build it next.
            Want early access?" + email field + "No thanks" that closes the box.
MEASURE     views of the option → clicks → emails left.
COMPARE     to a similar existing option on the same page, same period.
```

Rules: say "not live yet" immediately. Never take payment. Remove the door when the test ends, or ship the feature.

## 4 · Pre-sale offer

```
WHAT          Founding-member price or refundable deposit for [specific first version].
DELIVERY      Date you'll ship by. Be specific.
REFUND        "Full refund if we don't ship by [date], or within [N] days of delivery if it's not for you."
WHY NOW       A real reason: limited founding seats you will actually cap, or a price that will rise
              on a stated date. No fake countdowns.
PAYMENT       The methods your market actually uses (see mena-notes). One link.
SEQUENCE      Email/WhatsApp the waitlist: (1) the offer and the story, (2) answers to real objections,
              (3) a reminder before the deadline, (4) last call. Then the paid audience.
```

## 5 · Waitlist follow-up (between Step 2 and Step 4)

1. **Welcome (immediately):** thanks, what happens next, and one question about their situation.
2. **Progress (week 1–2):** what you learned from the interviews, a sketch or demo, and an invitation to a 15-minute call.
3. **Pre-sale (when ready):** the offer from §4.

Keep a list of everyone who replies. They're your next interviewees.
