# Benchmarks this skill may quote

These are the **only** reference numbers the skill should quote. Each one is copied from the sourced files in Growth Marketing OS [`benchmarks/`](https://github.com/growthack88/growth-marketing-os/tree/main/benchmarks), with its source, year and caveat. Use them as **sanity checks next to a pass line computed from the user's economics**, never as targets or predictions. For anything not listed here, say there's no sourced number and show how to measure it.

| What | Number | Source | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median landing-page conversion, all industries | 6.6% | Unbounce, Q4 2024 data (41k pages, 464M visits) — https://unbounce.com/average-conversion-rates-landing-pages/ | Campaign/lead conversions; not comparable to site-wide purchase rate. A waitlist for an unbuilt product is a different offer. |
| Median e-commerce landing-page conversion | 4.2% | Unbounce Conversion Benchmark Report — https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/ | Same definition caveat as above. |
| Meta traffic-campaign CPC (US SMB medians) | $0.70 | WordStream/LocaliQ 2025 — https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2025 | US data. Never use it as a MENA CPC; measure your own. |
| Meta lead-form conversion (US SMB medians) | 7.72% | WordStream/LocaliQ 2025 (same link) | Lead forms on-platform, not landing pages. |
| Meta e-com CPM by market | US $16.08 · Qatar $12.67 · KSA $12.01 · UAE $10.00 · Kuwait $8.07 · Egypt $1.81 | Lebesgue country CPM data, 2026 — https://lebesgue.io/facebook-ads/facebook-cpm-by-country | Methodology undisclosed: directional only. Shows that reach costs differ by large multiples between countries. |
| MENA CPC benchmarks | none reliable | Growth Marketing OS paid-ads benchmarks note | "Anyone quoting exact MENA CPCs is quoting their own account." Measure with a pilot. |
| B2B SaaS free → paid by model | opt-in trial 4–6% good / 10–15% great · card-required trial 25–35% good / 50–60% great · freemium 3–5% good / 8–12% great | Growth Unhinged Free-to-Paid Conversion Report, Jan 2026 — https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/free-to-paid-conversion-report | Match the model before comparing (opt-in vs. card-required differ about 6×). |
| Consumer subscription apps download → paid (median) | hard paywall 12.1% · freemium 2.2% | RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2025 — https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps/ | Apps only; real transaction data. |

When quoting, always say "sourced benchmark", give the source and year, and add the caveat in one line.
