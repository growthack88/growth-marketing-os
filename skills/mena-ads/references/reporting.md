# Reporting — weekly updates and monthly client reports (AR / EN)

Load for `report`. A report exists to drive a decision, not to show activity. Every report answers: what changed, why, what we're doing about it, what needs approval, and what we're deliberately not changing yet.

---

## 1 · Rules

- **Report a metric move only if it's real:** ≥ ±15% with ≥ 30 conversions/week (or ≥ 1,000 clicks); at lower volume use ±25% against a 4-week average. Always report spend changes.
- **Three truths side by side:** platform-reported, backend (store/CRM), and for COD, delivered. State the attribution window used.
- **Blended view first, platforms second:** total spend, total revenue (backend), blended MER / CAC, then per-platform detail.
- **Confidence on each finding:** High (lots of data, consistent) · Medium · Low (directional).
- **Language:** the client's language. In Arabic reports, keep metric names in English (ROAS, CPA, CTR, CPM), explain each once in Arabic for non-marketers, and use one digit style consistently.
- **Currency:** the client's currency, VAT basis stated.

## 2 · Weekly update (5-10 lines, Slack/WhatsApp-ready)

```
📊 [Brand] · week [dd/mm–dd/mm] · spend [X] ([+/-]% vs last week)
Result: [N] orders / leads · real CPA [X] (target [Y]) · real ROAS [X] · MER [X]
What changed: [1-2 lines, with the cause]
Done this week: [actions]
Next week: [tests + budget moves]
Needs your approval: [budget increase / new offer / creative]
Not changing yet (and why): [e.g., ad set X still learning]
```

## 3 · Monthly report

```
# [Brand] · Paid media report · [Month YYYY] · [Markets]

## 1. Summary (3 lines)
Verdict · the one number that matters (vs target) · the main decision for next month

## 2. Business results (backend truth)
| Metric | This month | Last month | Δ | Target |
Spend · Revenue (delivered for COD) · Orders/leads · Real CPA · Real ROAS · MER · AOV
COD: confirmation % · delivery % · RTO cost

## 3. By platform
| Platform | Spend | Share | Conversions (platform) | CPA | ROAS | Trend | Verdict (scale/hold/fix) |

## 4. What worked / what didn't (with evidence)
Top 3 creatives/angles (hook rate, CTR, CPA) · bottom 3 · tests run → results → decisions

## 5. Waste removed
Negatives added, placements excluded, paused ads, with money saved

## 6. Next month plan
Budget split table · tests (hypothesis + success metric) · seasonal moments in the next 60 days · creative needs

## 7. Risks & asks
Tracking issues · stock/ops constraints · approvals needed
```

## 4 · Charts that earn their place

- Spend vs real CPA by week (dual line) — the scaling story.
- Funnel bars: impressions → clicks → orders → confirmed → delivered (COD).
- Creative leaderboard table (concept · spend · hook rate · CTR · CPA), not pie charts.
- Platform share of spend vs share of backend revenue (efficiency index = revenue share ÷ spend share; < 0.8 underperforming, > 1.2 under-funded).

If the environment can make files, offer the report as a spreadsheet (data) plus a document or slide deck (story).
