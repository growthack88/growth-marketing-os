# LinkedIn · YouTube · X · Microsoft · Apple Search Ads · Retail Media

Load when the plan or audit includes one of these channels. Specs and minimums change; **verify** in each platform's help centre before quoting a number to a client.

---

## LinkedIn Ads (B2B, UAE/KSA/Egypt professionals)

**When:** B2B with deal value high enough to absorb CPCs several times Meta's; job-title / company / seniority targeting is the reason to pay the premium.

Structure and settings:
- Campaign Group per objective/market; campaigns split by **audience** (ICP titles vs company list/ABM vs retargeting), not by ad format.
- Audience size: aim for roughly 50k-300k for prospecting (smaller in the GCC is normal; don't over-narrow). Turn **Audience Expansion off** and **LinkedIn Audience Network off** for lead-gen until you've proved quality.
- Use **Lead Gen Forms** with 3-5 fields max, plus one qualifying question; sync to the CRM and send qualified stages back through the LinkedIn Conversions API.
- Bidding: start with Maximum Delivery to learn, move to Cost Cap once CPL is known; manual CPC only for small, tight ABM lists.
- Insight Tag + Conversions API live; exclude current customers and employees.

Creative: document ads and short native videos with a face; thought-leader ads (boosting an executive's post) usually beat company-page ads. Arabic creative for Saudi government-adjacent and family-business audiences; English for most UAE B2B.

Audit checks: L1 Insight Tag + CAPI live · L2 Audience Network and expansion deliberately set · L3 lead-form quality loop (CRM → CAPI) · L4 frequency < ~4/month on small ABM lists · L5 ≥ 4 creatives per campaign rotated monthly · L6 CPL judged against SQL rate, not raw CPL.

## YouTube (via Google Ads)

**When:** awareness and consideration at scale across the region, demand generation for e-com, app installs, and remarketing to site visitors.

- **Demand Gen** campaigns for performance (YouTube + Discover + Gmail feeds, Shorts); **Video Reach / Video Views** for awareness; **Shorts** inventory for 9:16 creative.
- Creative: brand and hook in the first 5 seconds (before the skip button), the product shown early, captions, and Arabic voice-over in the market's dialect. Make 6 s bumpers for frequency and 15-30 s for persuasion.
- Measure with view-through conversions *and* brand-search lift; YouTube rarely wins on last-click.
- Exclusions: kids' content, mobile games/apps inventory (accidental clicks), and sensitive content categories.

## X (Twitter) Ads

**When:** mainly KSA (news, sports, entertainment, launches, public conversation) and live-event moments; elsewhere in the region it's usually optional.

- Objectives: Reach, Video Views, Website Traffic/Conversions, App installs. Targeting by keywords, follower look-alikes, conversation topics, and events.
- Creative: native-tone posts in Saudi dialect, short video, timely hooks tied to matches, Ramadan series, national days.
- Weak spot: conversion tracking and lower-funnel efficiency; judge it on reach/engagement cost and brand-search lift unless the pixel proves otherwise.

## Microsoft Advertising (Bing)

**When:** Google Search is already maxed out and the audience is desktop/enterprise (B2B in UAE/KSA). Import Google campaigns, then fix bids and negatives. Volume in Arab markets is small; treat it as incremental, not core.

## Apple Search Ads

**When:** iOS apps in GCC markets (high iOS share among high-income users). Run brand-defence exact-match campaigns first, then competitor and category; Search Match for discovery with a tight budget. Track installs → trial/purchase via the MMP.

## Retail media (Amazon Ads · noon Ads · Talabat · Careem · quick-commerce)

**When:** the brand sells on marketplaces. In the GCC, a large share of e-commerce runs through Amazon.sa/.ae and noon; retail media is the "shelf" ad, closest to purchase.

- Start with **sponsored product ads** on the brand's own keywords and top category terms; defend the brand term against competitors.
- Judge on ACoS / TACoS (ad spend ÷ ad revenue / ÷ total marketplace revenue), not platform ROAS alone, and watch organic rank lift.
- Seasonal peaks (White/Yellow Friday, 11.11, Ramadan, back-to-school) need budget caps raised before the event, not during it.
